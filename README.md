**Godzilla vs. Kong** is a 2021 American monster film directed by **Adam Wingard**. A sequel to *Kong: Skull Island* (2017) and *Godzilla: King of the Monsters* (2019), it is the fourth film in Legendary's **MonsterVerse** and the 36th film in the *Godzilla* franchise overall.


## 📖 Synopsis

Five years after Godzilla defeated King Ghidorah, humanity lives in uneasy coexistence with the Titans. When **Godzilla** suddenly launches an unprovoked attack on an **Apex Cybernetics** facility in Pensacola, Florida, Monarch scientists and Apex executives recruit **Kong** from Skull Island to guide an expedition into the **Hollow Earth**—the primordial birthplace of the Titans—in search of a mysterious power source capable of stopping Godzilla.

## 🎭 Main Human Cast

- **Alexander Skarsgård** as *Dr. Nathan Lind* — A former Monarch geologist and Hollow Earth theorist.
- **Rebecca Hall** as *Dr. Ilene Andrews* — An anthropological linguist and Kong's caretaker.
- **Brian Tyree Henry** as *Bernie Hayes* — An Apex technician turned Titan conspiracy podcaster.
- **Millie Bobby Brown** as *Madison Russell* — A teenager investigating the cause of Godzilla's rampage.
- **Kaylee Hottle** as *Jia* — The last surviving native Iwi girl who communicates with Kong via sign language.
- **Demián Bichir** as *Walter Simmons* — The CEO and founder of Apex Cybernetics.
- **Shun Oguri** as *Ren Serizawa* — Apex's chief engineer and son of the late Dr. Ishirō Serizawa.

## 🥊 Major Battle Timeline

1. **Round 1 — The Tasman Sea:** Godzilla ambushes the naval fleet transporting Kong, nearly drowning him before Monarch cuts the ship engines to feign surrender.
2. **Round 2 — Hong Kong (Night):** Kong wields his energized battle axe to absorb Godzilla's atomic breath, knocking Godzilla down before Godzilla overpowers and dislocates Kong's shoulder.
3. **Round 3 — The Final Alliance:** Apex activates **Mechagodzilla** (possessed by King Ghidorah's neural consciousness), forcing a revived Kong and a weakened Godzilla to team up to destroy the mechanical Titan.
